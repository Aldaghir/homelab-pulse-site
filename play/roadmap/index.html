Roadmap

What Homelab Pulse is working on now, what comes after it, and what may come later. There are no dates here: each step ships when it is ready.

01 Now

The first public release

Version 1.0.0, being finished and tested.

  1. Everything in 1.0.0 in progress

    Your server’s health live, its containers and services, their logs, a terminal, alerts that reach you while your phone sleeps, access from anywhere and invites, in Arabic and in English.

    What 1.0.0 holds, line by line

  2. On Google Play with 1.0.0

    The edition with Pro, whose first feature, the file browser, is ready.

    What Pro includes

02 Pro

Coming to Pro

Listed as coming on the pricing page, with no date set.

  1. Advanced widgets

    A server for each widget, a preview picture, theme colors and an extra size.

  2. Extra themes

    Four designed themes, such as pure black and high contrast.

  3. A weekly summary

    Your servers’ week in ten lines, by notification or on Telegram.

03 Next

After 1.0

Decided, for the updates after the first release, in no set order.

  1. More ways to get alerts

    ntfy or UnifiedPush, a webhook signed with a secret of yours, and email, beside push notifications and Telegram.

04 Later

Being considered

Kept in the plan for later. They may change, or not come at all.

  1. Your server in more places

    On a Wear OS watch, in a PDF report, and to Prometheus, through a /metrics endpoint in the agent.

  2. SSH straight to the server

    A terminal over SSH, beside the agent’s own.

  3. Bigger homelabs

    Proxmox, TrueNAS and Unraid, and working with Home Assistant.

05 Not planned

Outside the plan, for now

  1. An iPhone app

    Homelab Pulse is a native Android app, made for Android.

  2. A dashboard in our cloud

    No account with us: your server’s data stays between your phone and your server.
