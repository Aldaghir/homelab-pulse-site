Press kit

Homelab Pulse in one line and in a short paragraph, its facts, and its logo and pictures to download.

01 Copy

In one line, and in a paragraph

Selecting any part of a text selects all of it, ready to copy.

  1. One line

    Your home server in your pocket: monitor and manage it from an Android phone through a tiny agent.

  2. A short paragraph

    Homelab Pulse is an Android app and a tiny agent you install on your home server with one command. It shows live health, alerts you even while your phone sleeps, manages containers, services and a terminal, and reaches the server securely from anywhere without opening ports. Arabic and English, no accounts, no ads, no tracking, with an open-source core.
