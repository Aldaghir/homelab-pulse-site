About

Homelab Pulse is built and maintained by one independent developer, Aldaghir, who publishes Android apps on Google Play.

01 The story

How it started

It started with a server at home. I wanted to know its state from my phone in two seconds, hear about trouble before something broke, and fix it safely from anywhere — without opening a port on the router or handing its data to a cloud service. I found no tool that did that and spoke Arabic, right to left, properly. So I built one.

02 Principles

What we stand by

  1. No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Your server’s data stays between your phone and your server.
  2. Arabic and English as equals from day one.
  3. An open-source core and agent: the app’s core under GPL-3.0, the agent under Apache-2.0.
  4. Funded by Pro, a one-time purchase in the Google Play edition.
