Pricing

Homelab Pulse costs nothing to run, on as many servers as you like. Pro is one payment in the Google Play edition, for features beyond the core.

01 Free

0USD, in every edition

Free, for everyone

With Pro or without it. No trial, no limit on servers, no account with us.

  • Live health, every two seconds
  • Up to 30 days of charts, on your server
  • Docker containers and systemd services
  • Their logs, live
  • Seventeen built-in alert rules, and checks of your own
  • Alerts by encrypted push, on Telegram, or both
  • A real terminal on your server
  • Access from anywhere through Cloudflare
  • Invites with a role for each phone
  • Backups, updates, fail2ban, UFW and Uptime Kuma

What each one does

02 Pro

4.99USD, once, in the Google Play edition

What Pro adds

One payment, yours to keep. No subscription.

  1. File browser available

    Browse, preview and download your servers’ files.

    Works on servers whose agent settings list folders to browse, from an admin phone allowed to browse files.

  2. Advanced widgets coming

    A server for each widget, a preview picture, theme colors and an extra size.

  3. Extra themes coming

    Four designed themes, such as pure black and high contrast.

  4. Weekly summary coming

    Your servers’ week in ten lines, by notification or Telegram.

What is coming arrives in later updates, and Pro includes it.

03 Buying Pro

How the purchase works

Pro is sold through Google Play, which handles the payment.

The price
4.99 USD. Google Play shows it in your currency before you buy.
Your account
Paid once, for the Google account signed in to the Play Store.
A new phone
Sign in with the same account and tap Restore purchases. The same after reinstalling.
No connection
A Pro purchase stays open for 30 days without one.
Paying later
When Google Play waits for a payment (cash at a shop, for example), Pro opens by itself once it goes through.
A refund
When Google Play refunds the purchase, Pro closes at the app’s next check with Google Play.
